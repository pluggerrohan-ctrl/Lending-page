Our Story

Built From a Phone. Built From a Problem.

Naman Bilthariya — Founder, Apexai

I’m Naman Bilthariya, a young builder, and I started Apexai with a simple goal: make website creation easier and more accessible.

I saw businesses spending thousands on websites, while many existing website builders were complicated, slow, or limited by credits and plans.

So I decided to build something different.

I built Apexai completely on my own, without a team, without outside help, and without even owning a laptop or PC.

I’ve been building it from my phone — learning, experimenting, fixing problems, and figuring things out along the way.

Apexai is still in development, but this is just the beginning.

I’m building Apexai to make professional website creation simple enough for anyone.
